
# Privacy Policy

**Application:** Teams OpenAI Test  
**Last updated:** November 24, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Teams OpenAI Test is a chatbot application developed by Pavan Kalyan for testing and demonstrating AI-powered conversational capabilities within Microsoft Teams.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you interact with the application.

## 2. Information We Process

When you use this chatbot, the application may process:

- Messages and questions you submit.
- Conversation context required to respond to follow-up questions.
- Technical information required to operate and troubleshoot the application.
- Information made available through Microsoft Teams when you interact with the bot.

Please do not submit passwords, financial information, confidential business information, or other sensitive personal information.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to:

- Understand and respond to your questions.
- Generate AI-powered responses.
- Maintain conversation context.
- Monitor, troubleshoot, and improve the application.

Messages may be sent to Microsoft Azure OpenAI for AI processing.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The application uses Microsoft services, which may include:

- Microsoft Teams for the chatbot interface.
- Microsoft Azure for application hosting and related infrastructure.
- Azure OpenAI for AI-powered response generation.

These services process information according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be temporarily processed or stored depending on the application's configuration and the services used.

Technical logs and conversation information may be retained where required for troubleshooting or application operation.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by the application. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your Choices

You can stop using the chatbot at any time. Avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information.
